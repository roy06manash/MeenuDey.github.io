About NGS Lab

Building the Future of Secure Networks

The Next Generation Systems Lab (NGS Lab) at IIT Guwahati conducts cutting-edge research at the intersection of 5G/B5G/6G communication, the Internet of Vehicles, UAV networks, and network security. Our work aims to build wireless systems that are fast, reliable, and resilient under real-world deployment constraints.

We combine classical networking techniques with modern data-driven approaches to address emerging challenges in next-generation wireless infrastructure — from real-time attack detection in 5G cores to secure V2X handover in vehicular networks.

Led by Dr. Moumita Patra in the Department of CSE at IIT Guwahati, with association to the School of Interdisciplinary Studies and Sustainability (SiSAS).

About NGS Lab

Building Secure and Smarter Networks for the Future

The Next Generation Systems Lab (NGS Lab) at IIT Guwahati focuses on research in modern wireless communication and computer networks. Our research covers 5G/B5G/6G communication, the Internet of Vehicles, UAV networks, and network security.

We work towards developing networks that are secure, reliable, efficient, and adaptable to real-world challenges. We combine networking and communication technologies with data-driven methods and machine learning to address important problems in next-generation networks.

Our research includes 5G network security and attack detection, secure vehicular communication, V2X networks, handover management, UAV-assisted communication, and resilient network systems. Through our work, we aim to make future communication networks safer, smarter, and more dependable.

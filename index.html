About the Lab

The NG Lab is led by Dr. Moumita Patra, Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Guwahati, also associated with the School of Interdisciplinary Studies and Sustainability (SiSAS). Our group works on 5G/B5G/6G communication networks, the Internet of Vehicles, and Unmanned Aerial Vehicle networks, with an emphasis on security, resource management, and real-time attack detection in next-generation wireless systems.

We have open positions for motivated PhD students. If you're interested in wireless networks, network security, or IoV/UAV systems, reach out via email.

Research Interests

5G/6G Communication UAV Networks Network Security Internet of Vehicles (IoV) Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning & Data Mining

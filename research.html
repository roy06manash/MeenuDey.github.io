Research

Wireless networks, Internet of Vehicles, IoT, 5G-based communication, and network performance analysis.

Research Areas

5G/B5G/6G Communication

Resource allocation, network slicing, and performance analysis for next-generation cellular networks.

Internet of Vehicles (IoV)

Secure and reliable V2X communication, handover management, and data dissemination in vehicular networks.

UAV Networks

UAV-assisted communication, deployment optimization, and anomaly detection in aerial network systems.

Network Security

Detection and localization of DoS/DDoS attacks, intrusion detection, and secure authentication protocols.

Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning & Data Mining

ML/DL-driven caching, anomaly detection, and adaptive resource management for next-generation networks.
